Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2024

Information We Collect

Earth Guardian is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect minimal information to provide our environmental news and monitoring services:

  • Usage analytics through Google Analytics (anonymized)
  • Location data for air quality monitoring (not stored)
  • Carbon footprint calculator inputs (processed locally, not stored)
  • Log files for system maintenance and security

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide accurate environmental data and news
  • Improve our services and user experience
  • Ensure system security and prevent abuse
  • Generate anonymous usage statistics

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. All data transmission is encrypted using SSL/TLS protocols. We do not store personal information from our calculators or monitoring tools.

Third-Party Services

Earth Guardian uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics for website analytics
  • OpenWeatherMap API for air quality data
  • Various RSS feeds for environmental news
  • CDN services for faster content delivery

Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access information about data we collect
  • Request deletion of any personal information
  • Opt-out of analytics tracking
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@earthguardian.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated revision date.
